Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans for most projects. We typically work with 50% upfront and 50% upon completion, with milestone-based payments for larger projects.

What about maintenance and support?

All projects include a support period (3-12 months depending on package). After that, we offer affordable maintenance and support packages starting at ₦15,000/month.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! As your business grows, we can upgrade or expand your solution. We'll credit your initial investment toward the new package.

What if I need changes after launch?

We offer revision rounds during development. After launch, minor changes are covered in your support package. Major changes are billed separately.

How long does each package take?

Starter: 4-6 weeks | Professional: 8-12 weeks | Enterprise: 2-6 months (depending on complexity)

Do you provide hosting?

We can recommend hosting providers or manage hosting for you. Cloud hosting typically costs ₦5,000-₦50,000/month depending on traffic and requirements.
