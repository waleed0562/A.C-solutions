Featured Case Study

E-Commerce Platform Transformation

Helping a retail business scale with modern technology

The Challenge

A growing retail company needed to expand online but faced limitations with their outdated e-commerce platform. They needed a modern solution that could handle increased traffic, multiple payment methods, and inventory integration.

Our Solution

We developed a complete e-commerce platform using React for the frontend, Node.js for the backend, and PostgreSQL for the database. The platform included payment gateway integration, real-time inventory sync, and a powerful admin dashboard.

The Results

  • 300% increase in online sales within 6 months
  • 99.9% platform uptime
  • 50% reduction in customer support tickets
  • Seamless inventory management
  • Multi-currency payment support
