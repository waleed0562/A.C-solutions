1. Introduction

A.C. SOLUTIONS ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website at acsolutions.digital or contact us.

By using our website or submitting information through our contact forms, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us. This typically happens when you:

  • Submit a contact or enquiry form
  • Request a quote or consultation
  • Apply for a job or internship through our Careers page
  • Subscribe to our newsletter (if available)

The information we may collect includes:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or organisation name
  • Message or project details you choose to share

We do not currently use third-party analytics tools, advertising trackers, or cookies for tracking purposes beyond what is necessary for the basic functioning of the website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and requests
  • To provide quotes, consultations, or project discussions
  • To process job or internship applications
  • To communicate with you about our services when you have requested contact
  • To improve our website and services based on feedback you provide

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Form submissions on this website are currently handled through the browser’s local storage for demonstration purposes and are not transmitted to an external server by default. When you contact us by email or phone, the information you share is stored only as needed to respond to your request.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect any personal data we hold against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or a valid legal process
  • When necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our business (only under strict confidentiality obligations), if applicable in the future

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Nigerian data protection principles, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information where we no longer have a legitimate reason to keep it
  • Withdraw consent for future communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@acsolutions.digital.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites (for example, social media platforms or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
