Our Story

Who We Are

A.C. SOLUTIONS, meaning "Anything Computers," is a Nigerian technology company dedicated to empowering African businesses through innovative software solutions, modern web design, mobile applications, and comprehensive IT services.

Founded with a mission to democratize technology and make enterprise-grade solutions accessible to businesses of all sizes, we've become trusted partners for companies across startups, SMEs, government agencies, schools, healthcare, and financial services.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class technology solutions that empower African businesses to compete globally, streamline operations, and achieve digital transformation through innovation, expertise, and customer-focused service.

Our Vision

To be the leading technology partner for African enterprises, recognized globally for delivering cutting-edge software, scalable systems, and digital solutions that drive sustainable business growth.

Meet Our Founder

Sa'ad Shamsuddeen

Founder & Lead Technology Consultant

With extensive experience in software development, system architecture, and digital transformation, Sa'ad founded A.C. SOLUTIONS with a vision to make premium technology solutions accessible to African businesses.

His expertise spans custom software development, enterprise systems, cloud solutions, and strategic IT consulting. Sa'ad is committed to not just building technology, but building partnerships that drive real business value.
